## Audience
Someone who reaches the essay from a link or a share card, often before reading a word. They should see at a glance that this is the same house as the case studies (a painted, classical object on a limestone ledge) and that the subject is memory and ordered places, not computers.

## Core moment
Virtual memory, a name space larger than the store with a map the system keeps, was designed two thousand years ago as the art of memory: fixed numbered places, erasable images.
As a scene: a row of fixed places with one image set in them, or a cage of birds you possess with one bird in hand.
Source: the essay itself (`content/writing/memory-palace.html`, sections 01–02) and its title.
